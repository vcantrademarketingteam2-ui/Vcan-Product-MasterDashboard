that powers Hermes; it is not a separate workflow authority.

### Authorization and brief handoff contract

For the one-shot pilot, the human approval is recorded by the Crew controller at dispatch time.
The controller must pass one structured authorization record and one complete approved brief to
the worker in its invocation context. The record identifies the repository, verified `HEAD`,
allowed write path, network scope, one-attempt limit, and approval timestamp or run id.

That controller record is the approval evidence for this pilot; a second approval file is not
required inside the source worktree. Claude must not search Obsidian, ask for another approval, or
infer the brief from a title. If the controller does not provide the record and complete brief,
the worker must stop with `missing_crew_authorization` before writing anything.

Obsidian notes are Hermes-only project context. They may describe status, decisions, tasks, and
evidence, but they are not worker instructions and cannot veto a fresh controller authorization.
The controller remains responsible for reconciling any stale note before dispatch.

## One-Shot Write-Capable Pilot Exception

Permanent automated worker dispatch remains disabled. After this reconciliation is committed and
a fresh full `HEAD` check is recorded, a human may separately and explicitly authorize exactly one
sequential, write-capable end-to-end pilot under all of the following restrictions:

- The authorization permits one attempt only, whether it succeeds or fails, and is consumed when
  execution begins. No retry is allowed under the same authorization.
- Only one worker process may run, sequentially. Delegation remains flat, and no parallel or nested
  worker may run.
- The pilot alone may use a detached worktree without a task branch. Its execution base is the
  current verified full `HEAD` recorded immediately before the pilot begins.
- The sole allowed write path is `docs/agentic-pilot/END_TO_END_PILOT.md`. It is a governance-only
  artifact under the `CLAUDE.md` governance exception and must remain unstaged and uncommitted.
- Only Claude authentication and one first-party model inference are allowed. Web search, web
  fetch, MCP, and all other external network access are prohibited.
- All pre-existing modified or untracked paths must be recorded before execution and preserved
  without deletion, overwrite, staging, or any other alteration.
- The pilot must stop before `git add`, commit, branch creation, merge or rebase, push, deploy,
  retry, permanent-dispatch enablement, rollback, or cleanup.
- Rollback and cleanup require separate destructive-action approval. Cleanup may use only
  non-force `git worktree remove`; `--force`, `git worktree prune`, wildcards, and recursive raw
  deletion are prohibited.

## Human Approval Gates

Explicit human approval is required before:

- Creating a commit.
- Merging or rebasing.
- Pushing to a remote.
- Deploying.
- Changing production configuration, Cloudflare/KV, databases, permissions, bindings, or secrets.
- Destructive operations, including deleting or overwriting user or production data.
- Promoting a learned workflow into permanent memory or a reusable skill.
- Enabling automated worker dispatch.
