## Current Operating Mode

Hermes is the sole workflow orchestrator and controller. OpenAI Codex is the reasoning driver
that powers Hermes; it is not a separate workflow authority.

**Product-local Crew routing is enabled.** Hermes may be opened from this Product Master
repository, but it remains an orchestrator and must not modify application files directly. Every
write-capable implementation task must be routed through the central launcher:

`D:\Agentic OS\crew\Invoke-ProductMasterCrew.ps1`

Read-only inspection, planning, and reporting may run in the source repository. After Hermes has
produced one complete brief and the human has explicitly approved its exact scope and allowed
paths, Hermes stores that brief under `D:\Agentic OS\crew\briefs` and calls the launcher with
`-HumanApproved`. The launcher captures the current full source `HEAD`, creates unique evidence
paths, and delegates isolation, dispatch, and enforcement to the central Crew controller. Hermes
must never invoke Claude directly or edit the source repository as a substitute for this route.

### Authorization and brief handoff contract

For every routed implementation task, the human approval is recorded by the Crew controller at dispatch time.
The controller must pass one structured authorization record and one complete approved brief to
the worker in its invocation context. The record identifies the repository, verified `HEAD`,
allowed write path, network scope, one-attempt limit, and approval timestamp or run id.

That controller record is the approval evidence for the task; a second approval file is not
required inside the source worktree. Claude must not search Obsidian, ask for another approval, or
infer the brief from a title. If the controller does not provide the record and complete brief,
the worker must stop with `missing_crew_authorization` before writing anything.

Obsidian notes are Hermes-only project context. They may describe status, decisions, tasks, and
evidence, but they are not worker instructions and cannot veto a fresh controller authorization.
The controller remains responsible for reconciling any stale note before dispatch.
